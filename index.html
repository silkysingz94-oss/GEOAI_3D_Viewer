METHOD AND DATA NOTES
• Enumerated population: enum_pop.
• Enumerated households: enum_hhs.
• Buildings: build_ls; mapping buildings: build_map.
• Compounds: comp_ls; mapping compounds: comp_map.
• Area: Area_sqkm, then Shape_Area, then GeoJSON geometry.
• Density: selected total divided by total displayed EA area.
• 3D height is a relative thematic extrusion, not measured building height or terrain elevation.
