The continuous colour scale is calculated from the currently displayed EA features. The same colour expression is used by the 3D layer.

METHODOLOGY
• EA attributes are read from the loaded GeoJSON properties.
• Population and household totals are aggregated from valid numeric values.
• Polygon area is calculated geodesically using Turf.js.
• Density is calculated as the total indicator divided by total area.
• Extrusion height is scaled relative to the displayed EA values.
• Colour classes are continuous and update with the selected indicator.
• Missing building and compound attributes are not estimated.
