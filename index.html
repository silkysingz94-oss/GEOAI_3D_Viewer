Methodology and interpretation

Indicators are aggregated from the displayed EA features. Population density is total population divided by total EA area. EA extrusion is a relative thematic visualisation and does not represent surveyed building heights or terrain elevation.

Enumerated values are prioritised where available. Mapping/listing alternatives are used where enumerated values are missing. Area uses Area_sqkm first, then Shape_Area converted from m², then geometry-based calculation.
