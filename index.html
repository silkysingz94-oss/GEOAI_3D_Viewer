Methodology and data notes

This viewer provides a web-based visualisation environment. The terrain surface is rendered from public elevation tiles. Thematic layers must be supplied from verified datasets. Map display does not establish statistical validity, positional accuracy, official status or completeness.

Use official GBoS, government and recognised partner datasets for administrative boundaries, enumeration areas, population and infrastructure analysis.
